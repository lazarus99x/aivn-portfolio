Questions

The ones people actually ask.

What is a “buying signal”? +

A real person, on a public platform, who posted something that says they're in the market. Not a scraped email. Not a job title. The words they typed and the date they typed them.

Is this just a lead list? +

No. A list gives you who. This gives you who, why now, and the line to open with.

What does it cost? +

Three prices, each spelled out with exactly what's included: $95 for the 14-day sprint, $300 a month for the retainer, $50 per booked call. See the offers →

What counts as a booked call? +

On the $50 plan a call is booked when the prospect accepts the calendar invite, and the invite lands in your calendar. Nothing else is billed — not a reply, not a maybe, not a no-show.

Do I pay before I see anything? +

No. You get a masked preview first — three real signals with names blurred — so you can judge quality before money moves.

Have you done this for anyone else? +

Not yet, and there's a card on this page that says so. What I do have is a logged run of my own tool with the real numbers. See the run →

Can I learn this instead of hiring you? +

Yes. Live sessions and real builds in the network, or Creata on its own if you just want the engine. There's a band at the bottom of this page for that route.
